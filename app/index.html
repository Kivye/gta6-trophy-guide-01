PLAN THE PLATINUM

Five stages. One finish line.

Structure-based plan · time band estimated from the list itself

Estimate from list structure: 18 of the 55 trophies are story beats; the tracked counters alone total 1,206 actions (250 stunts, 100 WAiNK locations, 100 vehicles, 50 challenges, 42 Shooting Range golds) plus $350,000 in cumulative money targets. A list weighted toward counters like this sits in the 100+ hour completion band — an estimate from the counts alone, not a measured or published playtime.

01

Follow the story

Gone Fishing → Chapters 1–4 → the final mission. Track the other named missions as they unlock; the finale earns gold, not 100% completion.

02

Missable-first precautions ACT DURING STAGE 01

Keep a Chapter 1 save and consider Lucia's balcony meditation. In Chapter 3, watch for the reported Race Against Yago contribution to stranger strands.

03

Explore & collect

Complete 3 card sets, WAiNK It at 100 locations, discover 100 vehicles, and explore Leonida. Revisit meditation spots and garage ownership.

04

Build the big counters

Finish 250 stunts, 50 Challenges, 20 different race wins and 8 stranger strands. Gold every Shooting Range challenge; track laundering and shop spending.

05

Clean up & audit

Finish partner feats, robberies and the remaining hidden unlocks. Resolve unknown triggers honestly, reach 100% Completion, then audit all other 54 trophies for platinum.
