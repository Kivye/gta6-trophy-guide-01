    How this ledger was made

    Every guide and step here is written in our own words from the pages listed above, read on the date shown. Where the sources agree, we say so once and cite them all. Where they disagree, both versions are printed side by side in the Missables & Disagreements view, with the edition's ruling under them — we do not silently pick a winner. Where no consulted source gives a method, the trophy is marked unconfirmed and carries no steps at all, rather than a plausible-sounding guess. Counts, hours and quotas are printed only where a read source or the official trophy description states them.
